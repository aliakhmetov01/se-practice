# User stories — Smart Campus study room booking

6 to 8 stories. Keep the shape exactly: ID, the As/I want/so that sentence, a priority, one
assumption. Roles are **Student** or **Administrator** only.

Delete the TODO lines as you fill them in — the checker treats a leftover TODO as unfinished work.

---

### US-01
**Story:** As a Student, I want to view which study rooms are free and when, so that I can choose a suitable room for study.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** Availability reflects whether a room is already booked or blocked.

### US-02
**Story:** As a Student, I want to book a free study room for a future time slot, so that I have a room for individual or group study.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** A booking of exactly two hours is allowed under R2.

### US-03
**Story:** As a Student, I want to cancel a booking I made, so that the room becomes available again.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** A student can cancel only a booking they made.

### US-04
**Story:** As a Student, I want to receive confirmation after a booking or cancellation, so that I know the action was completed.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** Confirmation occurs after a successful booking or cancellation.

### US-05
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to block or unblock a study room, so that rooms that are unavailable cannot be booked and can later be returned to service.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** While a room is blocked, it cannot be booked.

### US-06
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to review study room usage over a period, so that I can understand how the rooms are being used.
**Priority:** Low
**Assumption:** Usage can be reviewed for a selected period.
